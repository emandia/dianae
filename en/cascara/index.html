Fruit, taste, caffeine and preparation: the essential answers about cascara.

What is cascara?

It is the dried skin and pulp of the coffee fruit; the bean is the seed inside it. DIANAE uses cascara from Coffea arabica.

Is it coffee or tea?

It is a drink of its own: from the same plant as coffee and suitable for infusing, but not tea from Camellia sinensis. Its flavours range from fruity and sweet to herbal and slightly bitter.

How much caffeine does it contain?

The founder states 33–45 mg per bottle or DIANAE serving. No supporting laboratory report was supplied; the figure does not cover all recipes, since quantities, steeping times and coffee blends change it.

What does the energy feel like?

Consumers describe pleasant, balanced energy, perceived as long-lasting and different from other drinks. This is personal testimony, not a clinical comparison or a promise that everyone will feel the same effect.

How do I prepare loose cascara?

Use a teapot or infuser, a filter or French press, following the quantities and times on the pack. Mo leans towards black tea, fruity and slightly bitter; J is herbal and fruity, with notes reminiscent of mate.

Can I combine it with coffee?

Yes: ground cascara can also be prepared with ground coffee, in a portafilter or with other methods. Follow the product and equipment guidance; the blend changes flavour, extraction and total caffeine.

Is it suitable at any time of day?

It contains caffeine, which can increase alertness and affect sleep; individual sensitivity matters. EFSA’s information concerns caffeine generally and does not establish specific benefits of DIANAE.

Can I buy the new can?

The can is an unavailable concept, with fictional, untested ingredients and statements. For the availability of preparations already made, get in touch.

Why does the poll ask for an email?

You do not need email to vote: it only unlocks shared statistics and helps recognise duplicates. It does not subscribe you to newsletters or allow us to contact you; the contact form is separate.
