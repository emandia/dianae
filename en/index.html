What matters, beyond taste.

Cascara has practical, emotional and social value: enjoyment today, relationships and the world we want to help build.

01

For you

Taste and ritual: fruity flavours and different preparations for your own moment. Consumers describe pleasant, long-lasting energy as a personal experience.

02

With people

Growing together: recognise the knowledge of communities of origin and build direct relationships with coffee growers. Additional income for producers is a goal, not a quantified outcome.

03

For nature

Value the fruit: use skin and pulp as food when they might otherwise go unused. Any reduction in environmental impact still needs to be measured across the supply chain.

Antioxidants, digestion, satiety and memory are topics to investigate, not clinically proven benefits of these products. We do not promise weight loss or disease prevention.
